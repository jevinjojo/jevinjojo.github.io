Institutional RWA Compliance Protocol

AssetGate

An institutional-grade Real World Asset (RWA) tokenization and compliance protocol deployed live on BOT Chain Mainnet. Built on the ERC-3643 (T-REX) standard to enforce on-chain identity-gated transfers via ONCHAINID (ERC-734/735) claim registries and modular compliance rules.

Backed by an idempotent viem event indexer streaming state into PostgreSQL behind a high-throughput Fastify API, enabling 0-gas preflight compliance checks and Merkle dividend distributions. Protocol TVL is canonically indexed on DeFiLlama.
